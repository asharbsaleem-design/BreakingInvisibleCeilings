The framework

Five steps, one ceiling at a time

An Invisible Ceiling is a self-imposed limitation that defines how far you believe you are allowed to go. ARISE is how you break it.

A

ACKNOWLEDGE

Admit that something is holding you back. No blame and no excuses, just honest recognition.

R

REFLECT

Trace it back to where it started, and the label someone may have given you.

I

IDENTIFY

Name it in one complete sentence, so your ceiling finally has an address.

S

SHIFT

Take the smallest possible step this week. Movement breaks the pattern.

E

ELEVATE

Set the standards that stop the ceiling from sneaking back in.
